Block Blast 2048: The Ultimate Strategy Puzzle

Are you ready for a brain-teasing adventure? Block Blast 2048 is not your typical 2048 game. It combines the spatial awareness of block-dropping puzzles with the addictive math mechanics of 2048. Perfect for players who love logic games and strategy challenges.

How to Play Block Blast 2048

1. Aim and Drop: Drag the numbered blocks to the column where you want to drop them.

2. Merge Numbers: When two blocks with the same value touch, they will automatically merge into a single block with double the value (e.g., 2 + 2 = 4, 1024 + 1024 = 2048).

3. Chain Reactions: Plan your drops to trigger multiple merges in one move. This is the key to achieving high scores and clearing space on the board.

4. Don't Reach the Top: The game ends if the blocks stack up to the red line at the top. Keep merging to survive!
