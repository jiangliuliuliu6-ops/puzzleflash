📖 How to Play
🐍

Guide the Snake

Use Arrow Keys (desktop) or the D-pad / swipe (mobile) to steer the snake around the grid.

💡 The snake never stops moving
🍎

Eat the Apple

Red apples appear on the grid. Steer your snake's head onto the apple to eat it and grow longer.

💡 Each apple = +10 points base
⭐

Golden Apple

Occasionally a golden apple appears for 5 seconds. Catch it for massive bonus points!

💡 Golden apple = +50 points
💀

Avoid Walls & Tail

Don't hit the walls or your own snake body — the game ends instantly if you do!

💡 Plan your path ahead
⚡

Speed Increases

Every 5 apples eaten, the snake speeds up. Higher speed = higher score multiplier!

💡 Speed 5+ gives ×2 score
🐍

Level Up

Earn XP by eating apples. Level up to unlock harder grids and bigger rewards.

💡 Higher level = more XP per apple
🍎

Red Apple

+10 × speed points per apple eaten. At speed 3, each apple gives 30 pts. Speed 5 gives 50 pts!

⭐

Golden Apple

+50 × speed bonus points. Appears randomly for 5 seconds only — grab it fast!

📈

Speed Bonus

Every 5 apples increases your speed. Speed is your score multiplier — going fast pays off!

🎯

Length Bonus

At game over, earn +1 point per snake segment as a length bonus. Keep growing!

🔄

Hug the Walls

Travelling along the edges of the grid is the safest path — it gives your snake the most room to manoeuvre as it grows.

⭐

Chase the Gold

The golden apple is worth 5× a normal apple. Always redirect to it — but only if you can do so safely!

🌀

Spiral Inward

When the snake is very long, trace a spiral pattern inward so you never accidentally cut off your own tail.

🧠

Think 3 Moves Ahead

Before turning, always check: where will my tail be in 3 moves? Avoid boxing yourself into a corner.

Does the snake wrap around the edges? ▼

No — hitting a wall ends the game. This is the classic Nokia-style rule that makes every turn feel meaningful.

How fast does the snake get? ▼

The snake has 8 speed levels. It increases every 5 apples. At max speed the game is a serious challenge!

What is the golden apple? ▼

A golden apple appears randomly and stays on the board for 5 seconds. It gives 5× the points of a regular apple but disappears quickly!

Is my high score saved? ▼

Yes! Your best score, level, and achievements are saved in your browser and will be there next time you visit.

Can I play on mobile? ▼

Absolutely! On mobile a D-pad appears below the game. You can also swipe to change direction.
