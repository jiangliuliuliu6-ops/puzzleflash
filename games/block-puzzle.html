📖 How to Play
🖱️

Step 1 – Pick a Block

Three random blocks appear in the tray below the board. Click and hold (or touch-hold on mobile) to start dragging. On desktop, mouse drag works smoothly.

💡 All 3 pieces visible at once
🎯

Step 2 – Place on Grid

Drag over the 10×10 grid. A blue highlight shows where it will land — valid spots glow. A red highlight means it won't fit. Release to place.

💡 Block snaps to nearest cell
💥

Step 3 – Clear Lines

When any full row or column is completely filled, it clears and you earn points. Both rows and columns can clear simultaneously for massive combos!

💡 Rows AND columns clear
🔄

Step 4 – New Pieces

After placing all 3 blocks in the tray, 3 fresh random pieces appear. You cannot skip or swap — plan each placement thinking about the next 2 pieces too.

💡 Think 3 moves ahead
🏁

Step 5 – Game Over

The game ends when none of the 3 available pieces can fit anywhere on the board. There is no time limit — pure strategic thinking wins!

💡 Leave space for big pieces
⚡

Streak Multiplier

Clear a line on 3 or more consecutive piece placements to activate a 1.5× score multiplier. Keep the streak alive for massive point gains!

⚡

XP & Leveling

XP is earned from every action. Level up to increase the XP threshold. Each level requires more XP but makes you a better puzzle strategist over time.

1

Keep the Board Flat

Avoid tall stacks or isolated holes. A flat, even board gives all future pieces valid placements and keeps your options open longer.

2

Set Up Multi-Line Clears

Resist clearing one line at a time. Fill 2–3 lines simultaneously for exponentially higher scores. A triple clear (500 pts) is 5× better than clearing each separately.

3

See All 3 Pieces at Once

Before placing piece #1, plan where #2 and #3 will go. This prevents you from accidentally blocking yourself into a corner with no valid placements.

4

Corner & Edge Strategy

Reserve corners for small L-shapes and 2×2 blocks. Large pieces need open space — place them in the center or along edges with room to extend.

5

Clear Both Rows & Columns

Many players focus only on rows. But columns clear too! A tall 1×5 piece can wipe an entire column. Keep both dimensions in mind when planning.

6

Maintain Streak for Multipliers

If you can arrange your placements so every piece clears at least one line, you'll maintain a streak multiplier of 1.5× — this can double your total score over a long game!

Can I undo a move? ▼

No — moves are final, just like the original arcade version. This is what makes every placement meaningful and the game so strategically satisfying!

Can I rotate pieces? ▼

No rotation — pieces are fixed as shown in the tray. This is a core rule of the block puzzle genre. The challenge is fitting pieces in their given orientation.

How does Pause work? ▼

Click "Pause" (or press Space) to freeze the game. The board blurs and all interaction is blocked. Click "Resume" or press Space again to continue exactly where you left off.

Is my high score saved? ▼

Yes! Your best score, level, achievements and stats are saved in your browser's localStorage. They persist after closing the tab unless you clear browser data.

What are Achievements? ▼

Achievements are special milestones you unlock by playing — clearing 100 lines, getting a 5× combo, reaching level 5, and more. Check the Achievements tab to see all of them!

Does the game have a time limit? ▼

No time pressure at all! Block Puzzle Blitz is relaxing, strategic, and completely self-paced. The only limit is the board space available.
