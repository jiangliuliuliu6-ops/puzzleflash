📖 How to Play
🚀

Move Your Ship

Use ← → Arrow Keys or A/D to move left and right. On mobile, use the buttons below the game.

💡 Your ship never stops moving
🔫

Shoot Enemies

Hold Space to fire bullets at the incoming enemies. On mobile, hold the FIRE button!

💡 Auto-fire is always on
⭐

Power-Ups

Shoot power-up items to collect them: ⭐ Score Boost, 💜 Shield, 🔥 Rapid Fire, ❤️ Extra Life!

💡 Don't miss power-ups!
👾

Enemy Waves

Enemies come in waves. Each wave is faster and more numerous. Clear all enemies to advance!

💡 Waves get harder every round
❤️

Lives System

You start with 3 lives. An enemy reaching the bottom or hitting your ship costs a life. Lose all 3 and it's game over!

💡 Grab ❤️ power-ups for extra lives
⚡

Combo Multiplier

Kill enemies consecutively without missing to build a combo multiplier up to x4. More kills = more points!

💡 x4 combo = 4× score!
👾

Regular Enemy

+10 points per regular enemy killed. With x4 combo that's 40 pts per kill!

🔴

Tank Enemy

+25 points — slower but takes 3 hits to destroy. Appears from wave 3.

💀

Boss Enemy

+100 points — massive boss appears every 5 waves. Takes 10 hits to destroy!

⭐

Score Boost

+50 points instant bonus when you collect the star power-up.

⚡

Combo Multiplier

Hit 5/10/15 enemies in a row for x2/x3/x4 multiplier. Missing a shot resets the combo.

🎯

Stay Centred

Position your ship near the centre so you can reach enemies on both sides. Edge-hugging limits your ability to intercept fast enemies.

⭐

Always Grab Power-ups

Power-ups only last a few seconds on screen. Prioritise collecting them, especially the shield — it can save your run.

⚡

Maintain Combo

The x4 combo multiplier quadruples your score. Target single enemies to maintain the chain rather than shooting randomly.

💜

Use Shield Wisely

The shield absorbs one hit. Once active, play more aggressively — you can focus on scoring rather than dodging.

How does auto-fire work? ▼

Your ship fires automatically as long as you hold Space (or the FIRE button on mobile). There's no manual trigger — just focus on positioning!

What do the power-ups do? ▼

⭐ Score Boost: +50 pts. 💜 Shield: blocks one hit. 🔥 Rapid Fire: doubles fire rate for 8 seconds. ❤️ Extra Life: restores a life.

When does the boss appear? ▼

A boss appears on waves 5, 10, 15... (every 5 waves). It takes 10 hits but is worth 100 points!

Is my score saved? ▼

Yes! Best score, level, and achievements are saved in your browser automatically.
