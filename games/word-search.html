How to Play
Guess the 5-letter hidden word in 6 tries. After each guess, the color of the tiles will change to show how close your guess was to the word.
  • Green: Letter is correct and in the right spot
  • Yellow: Letter is in the word but wrong spot
  • Gray: Letter is not in the word
Type letters or use on-screen keyboard. Press ENTER to submit guess.
