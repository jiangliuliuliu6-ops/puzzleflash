Does the farm keep earning when I close the tab? ▼

Yes! Auto-farmers keep working. When you come back, you'll receive offline earnings based on your farmers' production rate (up to 2 hours of offline income).

What is the best crop to plant? ▼

For active play: wheat (fast). For passive income: grapes or oranges (high value). Mix them based on how often you click.

Is my progress saved? ▼

Yes, your farm progress saves automatically every 5 seconds to your browser. It will be there when you return!

What does Reset Farm do? ▼

Pressing Reset Farm clears all your progress. This is permanent — use with caution!
