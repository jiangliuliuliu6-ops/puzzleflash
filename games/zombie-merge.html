Evolve Your Undead Army in Zombie Merge

Welcome to Zombie Merge, the ultimate idle evolution game where you become the mastermind of the apocalypse. Your goal is simple but addictive: combine identical zombies to discover new, more powerful species and build an unstoppable horde.

How to Play & Controls

1. Drag and Merge

Use your mouse or finger to drag one zombie onto another of the same level.

2. Unlock Mutations

Every successful merge creates a higher-level zombie with better stats and cooler looks.

3. Collect Coins

Your zombies automatically generate income. Use these coins to buy more base units from the shop.

Game Features

  • Infinite Evolution: Dozens of unique zombie types to unlock and collect.
  • Idle Progression: Earn coins even while you are away from the game.
  • Strategic Upgrades: Manage your grid space and prioritize which evolutions to pursue.
  • Smooth HTML5 Experience: Play instantly on mobile, tablet, or desktop with no downloads.

Pro Tips for Success

To maximize your efficiency in Zombie Merge, always keep your grid as full as possible to ensure constant coin generation. Don't forget to check the shop for "Special Deals" to speed up your evolution process!
